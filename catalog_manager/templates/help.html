Catalog Manager — User Guide

Everything you need to process products quickly and publish them to Shopify.

1. What is Catalog Manager?

Catalog Manager is a tool built to help you process and publish your company's product catalog to your online store. You work through each product one by one - adding photos, writing a description, setting a price, and marking it as done. Once complete, products are pushed directly to Shopify.

2. The Dashboard (Main Page)

The dashboard shows all 2036 products in your catalog. At the top you will see four stat cards showing:

  • Total Products: the full catalog count
  • Done: how many products you have completed
  • Pending: how many still need attention
  • % Complete: your overall progress

The green progress bar shows your completion at a glance.

Use the filters to find products quickly:

  • Search bar: type any stock code, name or category
  • Category filter: show only one category at a time
  • Status filter: choose All Products, Pending only, Done only, or Pushed to Shopify

Each product row shows:

  • A small photo thumbnail (grey placeholder if no photo yet)
  • Stock Code (the unique product identifier)
  • Product name
  • Category
  • Number of photos added
  • Status (Pending or Done)
  • Shopify status (whether it has been pushed to Shopify)
  • Open button to work on that product

3. How to Complete a Product

Step 1 - Open the product

Click the green Open button on any product row.

Step 2 - Add photos

You have three ways to add photos:

Option A: iPhone or mobile phone (recommended)

  • On your phone open Safari and go to the app address (ask your IT person for the address)
  • Open the same product on your phone
  • Tap the "Drag photos here or click to browse" area
  • Your phone camera opens - take the photo
  • The photo uploads instantly and appears on the laptop screen
  • You can take multiple photos this way

Option B: Camera inbox (professional camera)

  • Your camera must be set up to save photos to the camera_inbox folder (ask IT for setup help)
  • Take a photo with your camera
  • Within 2 seconds it appears in the Camera Inbox tray
  • Click the green "Add to product" button
  • Click the thumbnail to see a full size preview first

Option C: Files from the design office

  • Save the photo file anywhere on your PC
  • Drag it directly onto the "Drag photos here" area
  • Or click the area to browse and select the file
  • Multiple files can be added at once

Photo tips:

  • The FIRST photo in the grid is the main product image on Shopify - make it your best photo
  • Drag thumbnails to reorder them
  • Click any thumbnail to see it full size
  • Hover over a thumbnail and click X to delete it
  • All backgrounds are automatically made white when a photo is uploaded - no action needed from you

Step 3 - Write a description

Click in the Web Description box and write a customer-friendly description of the product.

To save time use the AI Write Description button:

  • Click "AI Write Description" next to the description box
  • The AI will automatically write a professional description based on the product name and category
  • Review the text and edit anything you want to change
  • The AI is a starting point - always review before saving

Step 4 - Set the price

  • Sell Price: the price customers pay (required)
  • Compare Price: optional - shows as a strikethrough price on Shopify to show a "was/now" discount. Example: Compare Price R350, Sell Price R300 shows as "~~R350~~ R300" on the store

Step 5 - Check the Shopify SKU

The SKU is automatically generated from your category and stock code. Example: ELE-000-101 for an Electrical product. You can click Regenerate if needed.

Step 6 - Mark as Done

When you have added photos, written a description and set the price - click the status button that says "Click here when product is complete -> Mark as DONE". It will turn green and show "✓ DONE - Ready for Shopify".

Step 7 - Save

Click the green Save Product button. The app will return you to the dashboard automatically. The product now shows a green DONE badge.

4. Changing a Product After Saving

Every product can always be edited - nothing is ever locked. Simply click Open on any product, make your changes, and click Save Product again. You can add more photos, change the description, update the price, or change the status back to Pending at any time.

5. Photo Tips and Guidelines

  • Take photos against any background - the app automatically removes the background and replaces it with clean white
  • First time this runs it may take a moment to process (the AI model loads once and is fast after that)
  • Try to photograph products from the front, at eye level, with good lighting
  • Multiple photos per product are encouraged - show different angles
  • The first photo in the grid is the main Shopify image
  • Drag photos to reorder them - put your best shot first
  • Click any photo to see it full size before deciding to keep or delete it

6. Pushing Products to Shopify

Once your Shopify API key has been configured by IT:

To push a single product:

  • Open the product
  • Scroll down to the Shopify section
  • Click "Push to Shopify"
  • Wait for the green confirmation

To push all completed products at once:

  • Go to the dashboard
  • Click the "Push all done products" button (top right)
  • A progress bar shows each product being pushed
  • Photos, descriptions, prices and SKUs all upload automatically
  • Do not close the app while pushing

After pushing:

  • The Shopify column on the dashboard shows a green dot
  • The product page shows "Pushed to Shopify ✓" with date
  • If you edit a product after pushing, use "Re-push" to update it on Shopify
